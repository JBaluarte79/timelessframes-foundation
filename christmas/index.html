Their story

Deep in the Peruvian Amazon, near Iquitos, Bora families live along the river, far from stores and roads. For many children there, Christmas comes and goes with nothing to unwrap.

From December 12 to 20, 2026, our team is traveling there to change that. We will carry gifts, school supplies and basic aid by river to the communities.

We are starting from zero. Every donation, big or small, decides how many children we can reach.
