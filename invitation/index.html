The mission & the idea

Some things you cannot un‑see.

We built this journey for people who are used to making things happen.

The Timeless Frames Foundation is a 501(c)(3) from Greater Tampa Bay, Florida, with one obsession: no child should ever feel forgotten. This December we take a small circle of Tampa's business owners and leaders deep into the Peruvian Amazon. Not to look. To kneel down, put a gift in a child's hands, and listen to the people who live there.

You will travel in luxury. That part is easy. The hard part, the part that matters, is what happens when you come home and cannot forget what you saw.

And this is not a one-time gift. The Founding Circle exists to transform these communities for good: schools, food, books, technology and satellite internet that stay long after the boats leave. Everything we build is designed to become self-sustaining and owned by the communities themselves. Built to last. Owned by them.

You arrive as a guest. You leave as one of the people who changed their lives forever.

Why this matters

Life on the Momón River

The Bora, Kukama and Yagua families of the Nanay–Momón corridor live along the rivers outside Iquitos. Many children here grow up far from the Christmas most of us take for granted.

I

Distance

San Andrés, Padre Cocha and Nueva Vida are reached by boat, far from roads and stores.

II

Culture under pressure

The Bora of San Andrés keep their dances alive in the maloca. Tourism has become their lifeline: in a 2017 study of Padre Cocha, San Andrés and Manacamiri, every surveyed family worked in it, yet 37.9% said it left them no better off.1 In the nearby Nanay basin, illegal gold dredges are poisoning the fish families live on: 79% of residents tested carry mercury above the WHO limit.2

III

Children’s needs

Loreto has the highest child stunting in Peru: 20.8% of children under five.3 Nearly half of children aged 6 to 35 months, 45.6%, are anemic.3 In its Indigenous bilingual schools, 88% of fourth graders have not reached basic reading.4 And 43% of the region lives in poverty.5

Into the Amazon

Morning flight to Iquitos, then by boat to Irapay Amazon Lodge on the Momón River at Padre Cocha, our operations base. Sunset on the river.

San Andrés Bora community

Gift giving with Jose Baluarte: traditional dances in the maloca, time with the children and their families.

Monkey Island, Padre Cocha & Nueva Vida

Signature experiences

Fifteen moments that change you

Proposed, to be refined
01

Lima to the Amazon, privately

Proposed private charter: no terminals, no queues. One morning you're above the Pacific, by lunch you're over the endless green.

02

Arrival at golden hour

A private speedboat up the Momón as the river turns to molten gold. Irapay appears around the last bend.

03

Sunrise through the flooded forest

A silent canoe gliding between drowned trees while the forest wakes up around you.

04

Christmas morning, hand in hand with Jose

Not a photo op. A child, a gift, a moment of eye contact you will carry for the rest of your life.

05

Dinner on the water

A floating deck, a private chef, the Southern Cross overhead and the jungle singing in the dark.

06

Christmas Eve in the maloca

Candlelight under a palm-thatched roof. The most intimate Christmas Eve table you will ever sit at, if the community invites us.

07

The pink dolphins

Legends of the river. They surface without warning, and nobody on the boat says a word.

08

Monkey Island

Rescued primates at La Isla de los Monos. Expect curiosity, chaos and no respect for your sunglasses.

09

After dark: the night expedition

Flashlights on the water, eyes glowing back. A guide reads the jungle's night sounds like a language.

10

An evening of Bora culture

Dances, music and artisans. Every piece you take home goes straight into a family's hands.

11

A book for every child

Each guest writes a personal note inside. Somewhere on the river, a child keeps your words.

12

A private table in Lima

Proposed: an exclusive evening at Central or Maido, two of the most celebrated kitchens on earth.

13

Pisco above the city

A rooftop pisco masterclass as the lights of the Costa Verde come on below you.

14 · Optional

Lima's coast from the sky

Optional: a helicopter flight over the cliffs of Miraflores, or a day escape to the Paracas coast.

15

The Million Night: the farewell gala

Our last night in Lima becomes a night for the history of the river. Two special guests, icons of business and entertainment, to be announced, share their vision of the future, then lead a live pledge moment with one goal: another $500,000 or more in a single evening, taking the Founding Circle toward $1,000,000. Names revealed only to guests. And each of you receives a framed portrait of the moment you gave a gift. That's what Timeless Frames means.
