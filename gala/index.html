Why we gather

Some evenings echo for years.

The Timeless Frames Foundation is a 501(c)(3) from Tampa, Florida, with one obsession: no child should ever feel forgotten. Along the Momón River outside Iquitos, in the Peruvian Amazon, Bora, Kukama and Yagua children grow up reached only by boat, far from schools, books and the tools every child in Tampa takes for granted.

This gala brings the leaders of Tampa together to change that, not for one Christmas, but for good.
