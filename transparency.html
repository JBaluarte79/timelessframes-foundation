How donations are used

Donations support our programs as described in our mission:

  • Revive Smiles: our annual Christmas aid program bringing joy and support to children in impoverished areas, including Bora Tribe children in the Peruvian Amazon.
  • Empowering artisans: online platforms connecting artisans with global markets, and skills in business management, digital literacy, and sustainable practices.
  • Essential resources: food, clothing, educational materials, and medical supplies.
  • Living heritage: documenting oral histories, recording traditional music and dance, and preserving artifacts.
  • Dreams Through Arts & Sports: a new initiative helping kids around the world reach their goals through the arts and sports.
